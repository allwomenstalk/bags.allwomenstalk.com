# 8 Fab Studded Designer Bags to Elevate Your Closet

Well, I’ve written a whole post on studded shoes, so leaving you without a selection of fab studded bags to complement your favorite footwear would be totally unfair! Studded designer bags are trendy, cool, and versatile enough to instantly elevate any outfit. Whether you are searching for an everyday just fab purse or high-end fab designer bags, these hardware-accented pieces add just the right amount of edge. Here are 8 fab studded bags that deserve a spot in your collection!

- **Statement Clutches:** Compact, chic, and ideal for evening events.
- **Everyday Hobos & Totes:** Spacious options for daily essentials.
- **Hands-Free Crossbodies:** Practical styles that balance comfort and edge.
