# How to Build the Best Handbag Capsule Collection for Every Budget

I'm going to tell you the best handbags to include in a curated [capsule bag collection](#1). The one accessory that elevates any outfit—from loose linen pants and a crop top to a sleek, chic dress—is a fabulous handbag. Building a functional handbag capsule wardrobe ensures you always have the perfect purse for every occasion without cluttering your closet. In this article, I will share how to pick the best handbags to include in a capsule collection that fits your lifestyle and budget.

## 1. What is a Capsule Collection?

I browse Purseforum almost every day, and I came across an inspiring discussion regarding a handbag capsule wardrobe. A capsule collection is a group of essentials that fits your lifestyle perfectly. It can include as few or as many items as you would like, and you can have a capsule collection for handbags, shoes, jewelry, and even your overall wardrobe. The core philosophy is quality, versatility, and intentionality.

## 2. What Handbags do I Need?

A handbag collection is one hundred percent subjective because it depends on numerous factors, including the number of items you prefer to carry, your daily routine, your sense of style, and your personal interest level. This article is merely my opinion on the best handbag styles, and if you love a bag that is absent from my list, by all means, purchase it. The goal of the capsule collection is to only own items that you will use and enjoy in your life.

## 3. What Should I Buy?

I believe that every capsule collection should contain handbags that fit six specific categories: leisure, work, special occasion, runway, shopping, and classic. If you have a collection of handbags that fit these categories, then you will use every bag in your closet without fail.

Here is a quick breakdown of essential capsule wardrobe bag categories:

| Category | Recommended Style | Ideal Occasion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leisure & Casual | Crossbody Bag | Everyday errands, coffee runs, weekends |
| Work & Office | Structured Tote / Satchel | Laptops, office work, client meetings |
| Special Occasion | Clutch / Wristlet | Evening dinners, weddings, formal events |
| Shopping & Travel | Carryall / Backpack | Day trips, weekend travel, busy errands |

To keep your handbag capsule wardrobe practical and versatile, focus on these essential purse types:

- **Leisure & Casual:** Hands-free crossbody bags for daily errands and casual outings.
- **Work & Office:** Roomy, structured totes that fit laptops and daily documents.
- **Special Occasion:** Sleek evening clutches, envelope bags, or elegant wristlets.
- **Classic Investment:** Timeless handbag silhouettes that never go out of fashion.

For more details on selecting brands across various price points, check out our section on [brand recommendations for every budget](#5).

## 4. What Brands?

Once again, this is completely subjective because some people love designer brands and others don’t have a preference, so the choice is yours. The key is to choose quality over quantity regardless of the label. Whether you prefer high-street classics or search for pre-owned treasures like discontinued Tory Burch handbags, as long as you can afford it, it really doesn’t matter.

## 5. Brand Recommendations?

I will make recommendations based on budget to help you build your ultimate [capsule handbag collection](#3):

- **Under $100:** If you would prefer to spend under $100 for a handbag, I recommend [Target](https://www.target.com/) and [DSW](https://www.dsw.com/). Both of these stores offer beautiful clutches, totes, and everyday purses for great prices.
- **$150 to $500:** If you have a budget of $150 to $500, look into [Longchamp](https://www.longchamp.com/), [Tory Burch](https://www.toryburch.com/), and Rebecca Minkoff. These brands have sales, timeless designs, and a good variety of bags available throughout the year (including sought-after Longchamp discontinued bags).
- **Luxury Investment ($1,000+):** If budget is not much of a concern, then you can always consider iconic luxury labels like Louis Vuitton, Chanel, and Saint Laurent.

## 6. Conclusions

Your capsule collection is crafted by you and for you only. Just know that you don’t need to spend the most to look your best, and you don’t have to hide your love of designer goods either. Always buy the things that you want and enjoy your purchases.
